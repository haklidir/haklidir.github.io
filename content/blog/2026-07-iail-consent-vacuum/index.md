Osman Gazi Güçlütürk (Galatasaray University, Faculty of Law) and I presented **[The Consent Vacuum: When Agents Interact Without Humans](/publications/consent-vacuum-iail-2026/)** at IAIL 2026, the 5th International Workshop on Imagining the AI Landscape after the AI Act, co-located with HHAI 2026 in Brussels on 7 July 2026.

The paper uses the early-2026 Clawdbot/Moltbook incidents as a stress test for consent in open-ended agent-to-agent ecosystems and maps the resulting consent failures onto the GDPR, the EU AI Act and the Digital Services Act.
