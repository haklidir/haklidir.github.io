**[TR-TEB: Turkish Text Embedding Benchmark](/publications/tr-teb-lrec-2026/)** appeared in the proceedings of LREC 2026. The benchmark offers a standardized evaluation of Turkish text embeddings and reports results for 45 open-source models. The paper is available in the [ACL Anthology](https://aclanthology.org/2026.lrec-1.862/).
